# Note on confidential incidents

The disclosed catalog (`incident_catalog.csv`, `coding_matrix.csv`)
contains 63 of the 70 incidents used in the model. A further **7
incidents drawn from Space-ISAC member-confidential advisories** are
included in all aggregate statistics reported in the paper and in
`summary_statistics.md`, but are **withheld at item level** per Space-
ISAC disclosure terms.

Do not attempt to back these out from aggregate counts (e.g.
`70 - 63 = 7`) and publish inferred descriptions — the withholding is a
condition of the disclosure agreement, not an oversight. If you need
access for replication, contact the corresponding author about a
member-verification pathway.
